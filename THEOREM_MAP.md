# Paper-to-Lean theorem map

The current manuscript is **Analyticity of alternating-map functors and vector bundles**. Its expanded Main Theorem has four parts, with different verification statuses:

| Main result | Exact scope | Lean status |
|---|---|---|

Shared gate passed; functor construction and factorial classification have clean independent reviews. The combined-main and supporting joint-summary reviews are clean. |
| (2) Spherically complete targets | In positive characteristic, `Alt^k : Vec_K^op × Vec_K^∘ → Vec_K^∘` is analytic in every degree | Actual spherical-target bifunctor and joint finite power series proved in `alternatingSphericalFunctor_main` and its `_of_charP` specialization, including spherical output closure. The alternating action on operator-valued analytic bundle morphisms, exact coordinates and identity/composition laws are also proved and clean-reviewed. Here `Vec_K` consists of normed spaces with bounded linear maps, `Ban_K` is its full subcategory of complete spaces, and `Vec_K^∘` consists of nonarchimedean, spherically complete spaces. Regularity of the bifunctor means joint regularity of its action on each morphism space, with contravariance in the first variable. For isomorphism-closed domains in part (4), closure is taken inside the chosen ambient category.

The original formalized scope remains proof-complete. The extension now includes the checked finite-coordinate reflection and alternating-family theorems, the nonarchimedean radius refinement, core analytic-family API, invertible-shear counterexample, canonical exterior-support theorem, actual categorical functors with their regularity classification, and determinant coefficient spaces with algebraic padding and auxiliary specialization listed below. Remaining manuscript results and partial rows retain their exact scope in the pending table. Preparation alone is not counted as a Lean theorem.

The complete bundled operator entry point is [`AlternatingAnalytic.exists_banach_counterexample_full`](AlternatingAnalytic/MainTheorem.lean): the same Banach witnesses simultaneously have no bounded lift, nowhere analyticity, nowhere `ContDiffAt K ω`, and no equivalent ultrametric target norm. It follows from the unchanged construction and general lift criterion.

## Operator counterexample and classification

The complete counterexample declaration is **`AlternatingAnalytic.exists_banach_counterexample_full`** in [MainTheorem.lean](AlternatingAnalytic/MainTheorem.lean) (paper's numbered Counterexample theorem, `thm:main`). Its underlying construction is `AlternatingAnalytic.exists_nowhereAnalytic_banach_counterexample` in [Main.lean](AlternatingAnalytic/Main.lean).

For **every prescribed nontrivially normed field** `K` of prime characteristic `p`, including incomplete fields, and every `k ≥ p`, it produces actual complete normed `K`-spaces `E` and `F`. The target `F` admits **no equivalent ultrametric norm**, and alternating precomposition with `E′ = E` is analytic at no point. The same witnesses work for every finite index type, in any universe, of cardinality `k`.

The exact operator classification is **`AlternatingAnalytic.factorial_ne_zero_iff_allBanachPrecompositionAnalytic`** in [FactorialClassification.lean](AlternatingAnalytic/Analysis/FactorialClassification.lean) (Corollary `cor:class`). For every nontrivially normed `K` and every degree `k`, including zero, `k! ≠ 0` in `K` is equivalent to everywhere analyticity for every triple of Banach spaces. Its statement uses `Fin k`; the finite-index transport in [LiftCriterion.lean](AlternatingAnalytic/Analysis/LiftCriterion.lean) gives the equivalent formulation for any finite index type. The positive direction is stronger: [FactorialInvertible.lean](AlternatingAnalytic/Analysis/FactorialInvertible.lean), `ContinuousAlternatingMap.cpolynomialAt_compContinuousLinearMapCLM`, needs no completeness of the spaces and gives a finite power series at every point.

Here `Q(f)(m)(x) = m(f ∘ x)` is the actual continuous alternating-precomposition operator. “Alternating” means vanishing on repeated inputs, including in characteristic two. `HasEquivalentUltrametricNorm` means an ultrametric `K`-seminorm bounded above and below by positive multiples of the given norm; the lower bound makes it a norm. See [EquivalentUltrametric.lean](AlternatingAnalytic/Analysis/EquivalentUltrametric.lean).

## Added positive-case exposition

The current paper gives explicit proofs of the factorial-invertible case (in particular p > k) and of C∞ smoothness. The former is in [FactorialInvertible.lean](AlternatingAnalytic/Analysis/FactorialInvertible.lean). The latter, including smoothness reflection through a closed linear isometry, is in the pinned Mathlib modules `Analysis.Calculus.ContDiff.ContinuousAlternatingMap` and `Analysis.Calculus.ContDiff.LinearIsometry`.

The finite-coordinate operator proof and the finite-dimensional target reduction are additional informal results, now explicit pending requirements below. The finite-basis proof applies when either E or E′ has continuous finite coordinates; complete K guarantees this for finite-dimensional E or E′. It imposes no finite-dimensionality or completeness on F. This is a condition on the input spaces of the operators, distinct from finite-coordinate parameter reflection. The target reduction does not assert that finite-dimensional F alone guarantees analyticity over an arbitrary base.

## Deliberate exclusions

These are not asserted as mathematically proved results of the paper or Lean theorems of this package, except where the limitation below explicitly distinguishes the new proved special case:

- **Remark `routeII`:** the alternative infinite-field multiplier obstruction modulo finite rank and the route conditional on that unproved input. The support-dimension/contraction-rank equality in `prop:canonical-support` is formalized in [CanonicalExteriorSupport.lean](AlternatingAnalytic/Algebra/CanonicalExteriorSupport.lean); the independent obstruction remains unproved.
- **Remark `R`:** the proposed large-cardinal-index nonarchimedean-target counterexample using Erdős–Rado. The paper supplies no proof and makes no proved announcement.
- **Remaining analytic-domain questions:** the paper proves no largest full analytic domain exists over `F_p(t)` for all primes p and `k ≥ p` (`dom:no-largest`), but that theorem is not yet in Lean. The corresponding existence questions over complete fields and among Banach pairs remain open, as do the structure of inclusion-maximal domains and possible strict analytic extensions of the split-pair domain. Analyticity must hold jointly on every morphism space, including cross-actions. For C∞ smoothness, the full product category already suffices.
- **The other mathematical open questions:** intrinsic necessary and sufficient conditions on universal targets and on parameter spaces admitting every alternating-map family, countable-index ultrametric targets in characteristic `p ≥ 3`, and complete ultrametric targets over densely valued spherically complete bases. The paper's original request to formalize its operator counterexample is the objective addressed by this project, not a remaining mathematical open problem.
- **Serre's external classification cited in Remark `sorted`:** that every ultrametric Banach space over a complete discretely valued field is topologically isomorphic to `c₀(I)`. The sorted-lift theorem assumes the stated basis; this package does not prove that external classification.
- **The earlier characteristic-two development and its Pfaffian/parity proof:** cited for comparison and preserved separately, not re-proved or re-audited by this formalization. Its separate result proves degree-two nonanalyticity at the origin, with a wedging extension under (K2) to every degree at least two and every base point: `Round24Transfer.char2_not_analyticAt_of_hasEmptyNestedBalls`. This provenance detail is recorded here rather than repeated as a Lean identifier in the paper.

Infinite Ramsey, Ingleton's extension theorem, and the operator form of Main Theorem (2) are not exclusions: their needed statements have genuine local proofs or integrated proof sources as mapped above.
