# Isometric field and finite-index transport of lifts

An isometric field isomorphism L≃K₁ transports normed spaces, their completions, continuous linear/alternating maps, projective exterior norms, Q and bounded lifts without changing any norm or diagonal identity. A bijection ι≃Fin k similarly gives linear isometries of alternating-map spaces, conjugates Q for the two index types, and transports bounded lifts in both directions. Thus a single pair E,F with no degree-k lift for Fin k gives nowhere analyticity for every finite ι of cardinality k.

## Proof

Restrict or transport scalar structures along the field isomorphism while preserving underlying additive groups and norms. Its isometry makes normed-space axioms and continuity unchanged. Exterior powers are related by their universal property; the isomorphism carries every finite wedge decomposition to one of identical cost in both directions, so projective norms agree. Complete these isometries and transport Q by its evaluation formula. For index transport use m↦(x↦m(x∘e)) and the inverse bijection; permutation of factors leaves norm products unchanged, so these maps are isometries. Both constructions intertwine precomposition. Conjugating a bounded multilinear lift by these linear isometries preserves its diagonal, giving equivalence of lift existence. Apply the lift criterion for nowhere analyticity.
