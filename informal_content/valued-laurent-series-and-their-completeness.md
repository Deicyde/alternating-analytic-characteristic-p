# Valued Laurent series and their completeness

For every field κ, the Laurent series κ((X)) form a complete discretely valued field, with value group Z and monomial valuation determined by its integer exponent. The coefficient functions are additive and κ-linear; series have supports bounded below.

## Mathlib declarations

`LaurentSeries.valued`, `LaurentSeries.valuation_single_zpow`, and `LaurentSeries.instLaurentSeriesComplete` in `Mathlib/RingTheory/LaurentSeries.lean`, at the inspected local declarations. This supplies the valuation topology and completeness, before choosing a real-valued norm parameter.
