# Normed alternating precomposition

For a nontrivially normed field K, normed K-spaces E,E',F and a finite index type ι, define Q(f)(m)(x)=m(f∘x). Here f:E→L[K]E', m:E'[⋀^ι]→L[K]F and Q(f):(E'[⋀^ι]→L[K]F)→L[K](E[⋀^ι]→L[K]F). Alternating means vanishing whenever two coordinates agree. These are normed spaces; a complete target makes the relevant map spaces complete.
