# Nowhere-analytic counterexample over Laurent series

Let κ be finite of characteristic p, k≥p and 0<r<1. With K₁=κ((X)), E₁=ℓ∞(ℕ,K₁) and B the projective exterior completion, Q^{K₁}_{E₁,E₁,B} has no bounded k-linear lift and is analytic at no point.

## Proof

A bounded lift P would give the residue lift Ψ on E₀=κ^ℕ. It is multilinear, alternating and therefore antisymmetric in the vector slots, satisfies Pol1 and has a uniform support bound. Since p≤k, (k!:κ)=0, contradicting the finite-field multiplier theorem. The lift criterion converts nonexistence of a lift into failure of AnalyticAt at every continuous linear endomorphism.
