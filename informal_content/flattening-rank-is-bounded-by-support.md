# Flattening rank is bounded by support

Let V⊆L^S, ω∈Λ^kV and α one slot. For finite sets R⊆S and R'⊆S^(k-1), flatten Ωω into the matrix M indexed by R×R' by varying the α coordinate in R and the remaining tuple in R'. Then rank M≤sdim(ω).

## Proof

Take a supporting subspace W of minimal dimension and write ω as a finite sum of wedges of elements y_{j,b}∈W. Expand each determinant along row α. Each column function c_α↦M(c_α,c') is a linear combination of restrictions of the y_{j,b} to R. Thus the column space is contained in the restriction image of W, whose dimension is at most dim W=sdim(ω).
