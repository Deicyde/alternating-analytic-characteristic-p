# Isometric Laurent subfield in the completed base

Let K' be a complete nontrivially normed field of characteristic p and choose t with 0<‖t‖=r<1. Evaluation X↦t extends to an isometric field homomorphism F_p((X))→K' for the norm parameter r. Its image K₁ is the closed subfield equal to the closure of F_p(t); K₁ is complete, nontrivially normed and spherically complete.

Complete local prescribed-radius rational evaluation, polynomial norm identity, dense isometric ring-homomorphism extension, and actual closed Laurent image equal to the closure of the rational-function image. Constants and X have the required images. LaurentSubfieldProperties proves completeness, spherical completeness and nontrivial norm on the actual image subfield. PrescribedLaurentBase uses a lifted finite prime field to retain the prescribed universe.
