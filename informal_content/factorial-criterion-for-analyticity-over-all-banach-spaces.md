# Factorial criterion for analyticity over all Banach spaces

For a fixed nontrivially normed field K and finite index type ι, Q^K_{E,E',F} is analytic for all K-Banach spaces E,E',F if and only if ((card ι)!:K)≠0. In the vanishing case there are K-Banach E=E' and F with no equivalent ultrametric norm for which Q is analytic at no point.

## Proof

The nonzero-factorial direction follows for all normed spaces from normalized alternatization. If the factorial vanishes, K cannot have characteristic zero. Its characteristic is a prime p>0 and divisibility of the factorial by p implies p≤card ι. Apply the main theorem for this prescribed K and degree card ι. Its witnesses contradict the claimed universal analyticity, establishing the converse and the stronger nowhere-analytic conclusion. The empty index case has factorial 1 and therefore lies only in the positive direction.
