# A lift over the scalar extension descends to the subfield

Let K₁⊆K' satisfy the preceding hypotheses, E₁ be normed and F₁ Banach. Put E=E₁ completed-tensor-π K' and F=F₁ completed-tensor-π K'. Every bounded k-linear lift P of Q^{K'}_{E,E,F} induces a bounded K₁-linear degree-k lift P₁ of Q^{K₁}_{E₁,E₁,F₁}, with ‖P₁‖≤‖P‖. Therefore nonexistence of a lift ascends from K₁ to K'.

## Proof

Define P₁(f₁,…,f_k)(m)(x)=Pi_F(P((f₁)_{K'},…,(f_k)_{K'})(m_{K'})(iota_E x₁,…,iota_E x_k)). The isometries, norm-one retraction and norm bounds for base-changed operators/forms give the bound ‖P₁(f)(m)(x)‖≤‖P‖∏‖f_i‖‖m‖∏‖x_i‖. Scalar restriction supplies K₁-multilinearity, and strong alternation is preserved through embedding and projection. On the diagonal, use the lift identity for f_{K'}, commute it past iota_E, use the form embedding identity and cancel Pi_F∘iota_F. The result is m(fx₁,…,fx_k), exactly Q^{K₁}(f)(m)(x).
