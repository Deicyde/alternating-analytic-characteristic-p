# Restriction forms of the finite-field multiplier obstruction

Over a finite field L with (k!:L)=0, let inc:Vfin→E and out:E→V be linear maps whose composite is the canonical inclusion of finite sequences into all sequences. No actual doubly multilinear multiplier lift on E, antisymmetric in vector slots and satisfying the genuine polarized wedge identity on inc-inputs, has a uniform exterior-support bound on all inputs. In particular this gives both E=V and E=Vfin versions of paper theorem FF(2).

The proof restricts the genuine input maps to finite sequences and maps exterior outputs through out. Exterior support does not increase, so the verified ternary-input theorem applies. This includes both sequence spaces in theorem FF(2), with antisymmetry retained in characteristic two.
