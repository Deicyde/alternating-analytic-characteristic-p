# Unbounded unit-vector sums exclude equivalent ultrametric norms

Let K be a normed field and F a normed K-space. Suppose ‖v_n‖≤1 and ‖∑_{i<N}v_i‖≥N/M for every natural N, where M>0. Then F admits no ultrametric norm with positive two-sided comparison constants against its given norm. No completeness assumption is needed.

## Proof

Suppose q is ultrametric and ‖x‖≤Aq(x), q(x)≤B‖x‖ for A,B>0. Finite induction and the ultrametric triangle inequality give q(∑_{i<N}v_i)≤B. Hence ‖∑_{i<N}v_i‖≤AB for every N. Choose a natural N greater than ABM to contradict the lower bound. More generally, any unbounded sequence of such partial sums gives the same contradiction.
