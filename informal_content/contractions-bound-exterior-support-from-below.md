# Contractions bound exterior support from below

For k≥2 and φ=(φ₁,…,φ_{k-1})∈(V*)^(k-1), there is a linear map c_φ:Λ^kV→V with c_φ(x₁∧⋯∧x_k)=∑ᵢ(-1)^(k+i)det(φ_a(x_b))_{a<k,b≠i}x_i. It sends Λ^kW into W. Consequently dim span{c_φ(ω):φ}≤sdim(ω).

## Proof

The displayed formula is multilinear. Applying any ψ∈V* gives the determinant with φ₁,…,φ_{k-1},ψ as rows, by expansion along the last row. Repeated columns force this determinant to vanish; linear functionals separate V, so the original vector expression is alternating and factors through Λ^kV. Every summand lies in the span of the wedge factors, giving preservation of W. All contractions of ω lie in a minimal supporting W, which proves the dimension inequality. No equality between support and contraction rank is asserted.
