# Finite sums and permutation signs

Finite sums on finite index types, the symmetric group on a finite type, its sign homomorphism, and its cardinality are available. In particular the sign of a transposition is -1 and the number of permutations of a k-element type is k!.

## Mathlib declarations

`Equiv.Perm.sign`, `Equiv.Perm.sign_swap`, `Equiv.Perm.sign_mul` in `Mathlib/GroupTheory/Perm/Sign.lean`; `Fintype.card_perm` in `Mathlib/Data/Fintype/Perm.lean`.
