# Polarized diagonal gives the sum of cluster values

If Ψ is antisymmetric in its vector slots and satisfies Pol1 for coordinatewise multipliers, then ∑_{τ∈S_k}χ(τ)=1.

## Proof

Take C₁<⋯<C_k, u_j=s_{C_j}, v_j=w_{C_j} and c_j=C_j(0). Apply evaluation of the determinant array at c to Pol1. On the right all nonidentity permutations have a mismatched pair of disjoint supports and hence a zero wedge factor. The identity contributes det I=1 because u_jv_j=e_{C_j(0)}-e_{C_j(2)}. For each left term indexed by σ, reorder the vector arguments to match the operator order, contributing sign σ, and reorder output coordinates in the same way, contributing another sign σ. The product of signs is 1, including characteristic 2, and the term becomes χ(C_{σ(1)},…,C_{σ(k)})=χ(σ). Sum over σ.
