# Exterior support dimension

For k≥1 and ω∈Λ^k_L V, define sdim(ω) as the least finite dimension of an L-subspace W of V for which ω belongs to the image of Λ^kW. Every ω has such a W. One has sdim(0)=0, sdim(cω)=sdim(ω) for c≠0, sdim(ω+η)≤sdim(ω)+sdim(η), and sdim(ω)≤dim span S whenever ω is a sum of wedges using a finite set S. An injective ambient-space enlargement cannot increase support dimension.

## Proof

Write ω as a finite sum of pure wedges and use the span of all its factors. Its dimension is finite, so the set of admissible natural dimensions is nonempty and has a least element. The zero subspace handles zero. Multiplication by a nonzero scalar preserves membership in each exterior subspace. If W and U support two elements, W+U supports their sum and dim(W+U)≤dim W+dim U. The image of a supporting subspace under an embedding has no larger dimension and supports the mapped element.
