# Nonvanishing factorial gives an everywhere finite power series

For every nontrivially normed field K, finite index type ι and normed K-spaces E,E',F, if ((card ι)!:K)≠0 then Q is continuously polynomial at every point and hence analytic everywhere. It admits a finite power series of infinite radius at every point. The spaces need not be complete, and card ι=0 is included.

## Proof

For k=card ι≥1 define P(f₁,…,f_k)(m)(x)=(k!)^(-1)∑_{σ∈S_k}m(f_{σ(1)}x₁,…,f_{σ(k)}x_k). Norm bounds for each term make P bounded and multilinear. If two x-slots agree, pair σ with its composition by their transposition; the pair has equal factors in the swapped positions and opposite values by alternation, so the sum vanishes. On the diagonal all k! terms equal Q(f), yielding a lift. The lift criterion produces the finite infinite-radius expansion at every point. If k=0, Q is constant and the degree-zero series suffices. Transport along an index bijection as needed.
