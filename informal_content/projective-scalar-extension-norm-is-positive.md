# Projective scalar-extension norm is positive

Assume K₁⊆K' is isometric, K₁ is complete and spherically complete, and K' is ultrametric. For any normed K₁-space V, give V⊗_{K₁}K' the K'-scalar action through its second factor and projective norm inf∑‖x_j‖|λ_j| over tensor decompositions. This is a genuine K'-norm, without an ultrametric assumption on V. For every bounded K₁-linear ψ:K'→K₁, T_ψ(x⊗λ)=ψ(λ)x satisfies ‖T_ψu‖≤‖ψ‖‖u‖π.

## Proof

The contraction bound follows from the ordinary triangle inequality on each tensor decomposition and then taking its infimum. Concatenation and rescaling second factors prove the triangle and scalar norm laws. To prove positivity, express nonzero u as ∑_{j=1}^n x_j⊗λ_j with independent λ_j over K₁; some x_j is nonzero. The coordinate functional on their finite-dimensional span selecting this coefficient is bounded, since K₁ is complete. Ingleton extends that scalar functional to all of ultrametric K'. Applying T_ψ extracts the nonzero x_j and gives 0<‖x_j‖≤‖ψ‖‖u‖π. No bounded functional on V is needed.
