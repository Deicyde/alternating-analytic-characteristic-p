# Dense scalar restriction preserves maps and lifts

Let K be a nontrivially normed field and Khat its completion. For Khat-Banach spaces E,E',F viewed over K, the spaces of bounded K-linear maps and of bounded Khat-linear maps agree isometrically; the same holds for continuous alternating maps. These identifications intertwine Q. Every bounded k-linear lift over K consequently becomes a bounded k-linear lift over Khat with the same norm.

## Proof

For a continuous K-linear map g and λ∈Khat, approximate λ by λ_n∈K. Then g(λx)=lim g(λ_n x)=lim λ_n g(x)=λg(x). Apply this slotwise to each continuous multilinear map. Alternation is a property of the underlying function and does not change. Norms are determined by the same pointwise norm inequalities, so the resulting identifications are isometric. The pointwise formula for Q is unchanged. Its source and target spaces now carry compatible Khat-actions; the same density argument in each slot of a bounded K-multilinear lift upgrades it to Khat-multilinearity. Completeness of the original spaces is retained on restricting scalars.
