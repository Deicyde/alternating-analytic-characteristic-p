# Positive characteristic forces an ultrametric field

If K is a normed field of characteristic p>0, its norm is ultrametric. Every nonzero element of its prime field has norm 1.

## Proof

For a prime-field element c≠0, c^(p-1)=1 implies ‖c‖=1. All binomial coefficients therefore have norm at most 1. For x,y∈K and every N≥1, the binomial theorem gives ‖x+y‖^N≤(N+1)max(‖x‖,‖y‖)^N. Taking N-th roots and using (N+1)^(1/N)→1 gives ‖x+y‖≤max(‖x‖,‖y‖). The zero maximum case is immediate.
