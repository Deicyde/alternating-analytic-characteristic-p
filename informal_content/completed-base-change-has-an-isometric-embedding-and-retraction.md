# Completed base change has an isometric embedding and retraction

With the same field hypotheses, let Vhat=completion(V⊗π K'). The map iota_V:V→Vhat, x↦x⊗1, is a K₁-linear isometry. If V is Banach then the scalar projection induces Pi_V:Vhat→L[K₁]V with ‖Pi_V‖≤1 and Pi_V∘iota_V=id. Vhat is a K'-Banach space even when the original V-norm is not ultrametric.

## Proof

The single tensor decomposition gives ‖x⊗1‖π≤‖x‖. Conversely the scalar projection varpi has norm≤1 and varpi(1)=1; its tensor contraction T_varpi sends x⊗1 to x, giving the reverse inequality. The completion embedding preserves this equality. For Banach V, extend T_varpi by the complete-target extension lemma, preserving the bound. The retraction identity holds already on simple tensors and hence on the embedded V.
