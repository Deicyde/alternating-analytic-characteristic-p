# Sorted lift for an orthogonal basis

Suppose K is ultrametric, E has a complete orthogonal coordinate expansion x=∑e_i*(x)e_i, with ‖x‖=sup_i|e_i*(x)|‖e_i‖ and coefficients tending to zero in this weighted sense, and F is complete ultrametric. After ordering the basis indices, Q admits in every degree a bounded lift of norm≤1 given by the sum over s₁<⋯<s_k of det(e_{s_a}*(x^b)) m(f₁e_{s₁},…,f_k e_{s_k}).

## Proof

For any fixed inputs and ε>0, weighted coefficient decay leaves only finitely many index tuples with term norm≥ε. The family is summable in complete ultrametric F. Each term is bounded by ‖m‖∏‖f_i‖∏‖x^b‖ by the determinant ultrametric inequality and orthogonality. Thus the whole sum has that bound. The determinant ensures strong alternation in the x-inputs. Linearity in m and multilinearity in f and x follow first for finite coordinate truncations and then by convergence. On the operator diagonal, the alternating-map expansion in the ordered orthogonal basis sums to m(fx¹,…,fx^k), giving the lift identity without division by k!.
