# Base change of bounded endomorphisms

Let E=E₁ completed-tensor-π K'. Every bounded K₁-linear endomorphism f of E₁ induces a bounded K'-linear endomorphism f_{K'} of E with ‖f_{K'}‖≤‖f‖ and f_{K'}iota_E=iota_E f. The map f↦f_{K'} is bounded K₁-linear.

## Proof

On the algebraic tensor product use f⊗id. Applying f to each first factor bounds each decomposition cost by ‖f‖ times the old cost; take the infimum to get the norm bound. Extend to the completion. K'-linearity is apparent on tensors and passes to limits. The embedding identity and K₁-linearity in f hold on elementary tensors and extend by density and uniqueness.
