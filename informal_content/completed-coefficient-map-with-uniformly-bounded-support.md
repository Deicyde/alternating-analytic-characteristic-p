# Completed coefficient map with uniformly bounded support

There is a unique κ-linear η:B→Λ^k_κE₀ with Ω^κ(η(b))=coeff₀(Jb). It satisfies sdim(η(b))≤kM_r‖b‖ for every b∈B and η(W_B(y₁,…,y_k))=y₁∧⋯∧y_k for y_i∈E₀. No K₁-linearity of η is asserted.

## Proof

Approximate b by algebraic ω_n with ‖b-ω_n‖→0. Since ‖J‖≤1, eventually the determinant arrays differ by sup norm<1 and therefore have equal constant coefficients. Injectivity of Ω^κ forces the algebraic coefficient elements η_alg(ω_n) to be eventually exactly constant, defining η(b). Their support estimates and ‖ω_n‖→‖b‖ give the inequality. Array injectivity also supplies uniqueness and κ-linearity. For a wedge of constant Laurent sequences, its array already has entries in κ and coefficient zero fixes it; uniqueness identifies its image. The exterior codomain need not be completed.
