# A bounded analytic lift yields a polarized residue lift

Given a bounded k-linear lift P of Q^{K₁}_{E₁,E₁,B}, define Φ(a;x)=P(D_{a₁},…,D_{a_k})(W_B)(x) using coordinatewise multiplication operators, and Ψ=η∘Φ on E₀^(2k). Then Ψ is 2k-linear over κ, strongly alternating in the last k slots, has sdim(Ψ(z))≤floor(kM_r‖P‖) for all z, and satisfies the full polarized identity Pol, hence Pol1, for multipliers on E₀.

## Proof

The map a↦D_a is K₁-linear with ‖D_a‖≤‖a‖. Compose P, evaluation at W_B, and vector evaluation to obtain Φ; η is κ-linear and preserves zero, so Ψ is κ-multilinear and alternating. Every E₀ input has norm≤1 and ‖W_B‖≤1; thus ‖Φ(z)‖≤‖P‖ and the support estimate gives the natural-number bound. For b₁,…,b_m∈E₀ and x∈E₀^k, apply the lift diagonal to a_λ=∑λ_i b_i for all λ∈K₁^m. Expand both sides by multilinearity. The infinite-field vector-polynomial identity principle over K₁ equates every coefficient sum in B. Apply η to each coefficient equality. On wedges of E₀ factors η agrees with the algebraic wedge, yielding Pol. Polarization is performed over infinite K₁ before applying the merely κ-linear η.
