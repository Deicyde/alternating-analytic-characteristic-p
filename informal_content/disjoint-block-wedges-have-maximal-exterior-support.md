# Disjoint block wedges have maximal exterior support

Over every field L, for k>=2 and N natural, the sum of the N wedges of coordinate vectors at indices k*j+i+1 (j:Fin N, i:Fin k) in the full function space Nat→L has exterior support dimension exactly k*N.

## Proof

For each vector, contract against the coordinate functionals of the other positions in its block. Its own block gives that vector up to a sign; every other block vanishes because k≥2. Every supporting subspace therefore contains all kN independent coordinate vectors. Their span also supports the sum, so both bounds agree.
